# Oidua

Oidua detects the BPM of live recordings and deeply analyzes songs you pick. Instantly uncover key signatures, song structure, pace, loudness, and vocal, drum, and bass activity.

It is more accurate than manually tapping to tell the BPM, especially for fast songs.


### Main Features

- Live BPM Detection: Measure the tempo of live recordings in real time.  
- Deep Track Analysis: Inspect key signatures, song structure, pace, loudness, and instrument activities.  
- Built-in Playback: Play back your recorded audio and picked songs directly inside the app.  
- Live Chart Indicator: Follow playback in real time across your analysis charts.  
- Drag-to-Scrub Charts: Drag the indicator on any analysis chart to jump to a specific section.  
- Dark Mode Support: Enjoy a comfortable interface in low-light environments.  

Note: DRM-protected songs (e.g., Apple Music streams) are not supported.
